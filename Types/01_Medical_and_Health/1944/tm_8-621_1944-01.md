                                      Section I. GENERAL

1. SCOPE, a. These instructions are published for the information and
guidance of the personnel to whom this equipment is assigned. They
contain information on the operation and maintenance of the equipment,
together with illustrations identifying the component parts of the equip
ment. They apply only to lamp, therapeutic, mercury arc, air-cooled,
mobile, complete, 110-volt, 60-cycle, Medical Department Item No.
7126005, and are arranged in three chapters: Chapter 1, Introduction;
Chapter 2, Operating Instructions ; Chapter 3, Maintenance Instructions.

   b. All service parts are listed in the appendix.

2. RECORDS. There are no forms or records which are required to be
kept in conjunction with equipment.

14. MEASURING THE DISTANCE BETWEEN BURNER AND PATIENT.
Use the steel tape measure located on the burner housing. The intensity
of ultraviolet radiation varies inversely as the square of the distance
between the burner and the patient. For example, moving the patient
a distance of 30 to 15 inches from the lamp makes the radiation four
times as intense, rather than twice as intense. Moving the patient three

times closer would make the radiation nine times as intense. If this prin

ciple is not constantly borne in mind when positioning the patient, severe
burns may result from moving him a short distance closer to the lamp.

lamp.

    b. Check current source to see that current is available* at the receptacle

   c. Check line fuse to see that it is intact and of the, proper amperage.
   d. Burdick model only. (1) Snap the line toggle .switch to the "on"

   (2) Test to determine if current is reaching the burner, by placing

20. IF THE LAMP LIGHTS AND IMMEDIATELY EXTINGUISHES ITSELF.

If the lamp lights and immediately extinguishes itself, it is likely that

a fuse in the electric supply circuit has blown. In the event that this is
the cause of operating failure, replace fuse.

21. IF BURNER FAILS TO REACH NORMAL BRILLIANCY OR GOES
OUT. The burner should be replaced if it fails to reach normal brilliancy
or goes out and does not light after being in use over a period of time.

Section V. MAINTENANCE OPERATIONS

22. CLEANING BURNER. To clean the burner, snap the line toggle switch
to the "off" position, moisten a small piece of clean cloth with either

acetone (Item No. 1003000), carbon tetrachloride (Item No. 1127000),
or ethyl alcohol (Item No. 1048000), gently wipe the quartz tube with
the moistened cloth, and dry with a clean cloth. Never use rubbing

alcohol. The cleaning solvents are listed in the order of their preference.

If these solvents are not available, distilled water may be used. The
burner should not be removed for cleaning. DO NOT ALLOW the

fingers to come in contact with the quartz tube at any time. In the event

that they do, clean as directed above.

23. CLEANING REFLECTOR. To clean the reflector, snap     the line toggle
switch to the "off" position, wipe lightly with a clean   cloth slightly
moistened with water, and dry with a soft clean cloth.
