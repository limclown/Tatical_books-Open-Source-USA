                                               INTRODUCTION

             This subcourse is designed to give you a basic knowledge of simple circuits
that carry electricity from a power source to some kind of electrical equipment. With a
knowledge of these fundamentals, you will be able to make better use of electrical
equipment and to better understand future textual materials that mention electrical
factors in the function of equipment.

Subcourse Components:

This subcourse consists of programmed text.

         Lesson 1. Basic Electrical Circuits

subcourse:

       --Complete the subcourse lesson.
