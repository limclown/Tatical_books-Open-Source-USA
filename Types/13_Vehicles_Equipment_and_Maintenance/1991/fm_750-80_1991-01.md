             ARMY WHOLESALE MAINTENANCE MANAGEMENT

Effective support of our fighting forces requires a coordinated maintenance system. Neither the wholesale
nor retail communities can provide support in isolation from the other. This manual illustrates wholesale
maintenance and the wholesale/retail interface.
