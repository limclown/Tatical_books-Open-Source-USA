Do not move the rotator assembly and mortar baseplate as a unit; move the rotator assembly by its handles, and use

Do not stand in front of sleeve nut when removing it. The shock absorber may contain broken parts that would allow the

used without protective gloves, these chemicals may cause irritation to, or cracking of, the skin.
