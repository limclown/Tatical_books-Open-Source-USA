            a. Peacetime.
                (1) To preserve order among military personnel within its area

          of mission responsibility.
                (2) To render assistance and provide information to Armed Forces

                (3) To exercise preventive action for the reduction of incidents and

          offenses.
                (4) To conduct preliminary inquiry into all reported offenses and

          custody of such personnel, as appropriate, upon their release or discharge
          from the court.

                (1) To expand patrolling and the frequency of patrolling dependent

                (8) Establish information booths in all large transportation termi-

The authority of the Commanding Officer of the Armed Forces Police

Commanding Officer of the Detachment will provide the service com-
mander concerned with complete details in writing regarding all inci-
dents requiring disciplinary or reassignment action by the appropriate
service commander.

ordinating authority such changes as are necessary to provide uniformity
in existing off-base regulations in order to facilitate military law enforce-
ment and eliminate confusion.
