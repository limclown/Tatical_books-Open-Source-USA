units with a nuclear mission, operating under combat conditions

peacetime condition; or they may be distributed to FSL during

the height of combat.

   Who actually will be responsible for the movement of weapons.

is also unpredictable. Weapon movement may be performed by

the weapon system by interrupting the assembly or firing se-

weapons systems. Once a weapon system has been disenabled
by a PAL device, it can be enabled only by applying the proper
combination to remove the mechanical device, or enable the
electro-mechanical device, as appropriate.

control of. PAL materials. Commanders of the unified and

specified commands have amplified these procedures for PAL

operations conducted within their respective commands. De-
