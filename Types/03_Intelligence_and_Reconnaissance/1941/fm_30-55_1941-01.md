    The material contained in this manual has been secured
from many sources. It is the best material available, but
may be incomplete and, in some respects, inaccurate.
    This manual, containing illustrations with explanatory data
relative to German naval ships, is published for limited dis-
tribution. It will be used for instruction of officers and men
in. the appearance and general characteristics of German
naval vessels and will be safeguarded as prescribed in
AR 380-5.
