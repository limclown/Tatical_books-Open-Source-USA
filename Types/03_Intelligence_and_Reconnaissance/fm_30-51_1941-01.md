                                          SECTION I

                                                     GENERAL
RM 1. Purrose.—The purpose of this manual is to serve as
a guide in the identification of naval ships of the British
Empire. It is the best available, though incomplete.

     2. Scorpe.—This manual, containing illustrations with ex-
Planatory data relative to naval ships of the British Empire,
is published for limited distribution. When illustrations are
not given, blank spaces have been left so that they may be
added when provided.


---

                                                  MILITARY INTELLIGENCE

@ 3. OBSERVATION oF DISTINCTIVE FEeATURES.—a. Observers
must be able to detect quickly and analyze rapidly every pos-
sible indication of identity of naval ships. Some character-
istic visible features of individual ships generally indicative
of the class and, when noted in sufficient detail, of the par-
ticular ship are—

    General shape and size.
    Bow and stern.
    Funnels and cinder screens.
    Masts and decks.
    Superstructure:

             Bridge, and navigation bridge.
            Chart house and compass platform.
             Directional aerial,
             Tower, control towers, and conning tower.
             Searchlights.
    Primary and secondary armament.
    Torpedo tubes.
    Planes, catapults, cranes, derricks, and hangars.
    b. Figures 1 to 9, inclusive, illustrate characteristic fea-
tures of warships and show the location of armor protection.
    c. Flags of the British Navy are illustrated in figure 10,
and insignia of rank worn on sleeves of naval officers are
shown in figure 11.
