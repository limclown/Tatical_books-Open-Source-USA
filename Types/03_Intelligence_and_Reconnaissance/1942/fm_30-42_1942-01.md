                                                      SECTION I

                                       GENERAL
* 1. PuRPOSE.-The purpose of this manual is to serve as a
guide in the identification of foreign armored vehicles. The
material has been secured from many sources. It is the best
available but may be incomplete and, in some respects,
inaccurate.
* 2. ScoPE.-This manual, containing illustrations with ex-
planatory data relative to foreign armored vehicles, German,
Japanese, Russian, and Italian, is published for limited distri-
bution. When illustrations are not given, blank spaces have
been left so that they may be added when provided. The
manual will be used for instruction of officers and men in the
appearance and general characteristics of armored vehiclesand
will be safeguarded as prescribed in AR 380-5.
* 3. METHOD OF IDENTIFICATION AND CLASSIFICATION OF
ARMORED VEHICLES.-For method of identification and classifi-
cation of foreign armored vehicles see FM 30-40.
