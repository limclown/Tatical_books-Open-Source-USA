FOREWORD

Marine Corps Warfighting Publication (MCWP) 2-2, MAGTF Intelligence Collection,
builds on the doctrinal foundation established by Marine Corps Doctrinal Publication
(MCDP) 2, Intelligence, and Marine Corps Warfighting Publication (MCWP) 2-1, Intelli-
gence Operations, detailing specific doctrine and tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTP)
to conduct intelligence collection to support the Marine air-ground task force (MAGTF).

MCWP 2-2 is primarily for intelligence personnel who plan and execute intelligence collec-
tion activities. Personnel who support intelligence collection activities or use their results
may also find this publication useful.

unit’s daily intelligence summary (INTSUM) in order to notify higher, adjacent, and critically, lower units

update a unit’s collection priorities and collection plan daily. Using the basic format, units can tailor the
