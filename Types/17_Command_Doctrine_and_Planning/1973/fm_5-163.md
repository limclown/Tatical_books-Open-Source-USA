2–8. Facilities for Primary Treatment
  a. Bar Screen. A bar screen is used to screen

large particles such as rags, rocks and sticks from
the sewage. Bar screens are usually required at the
inlet to pumping stations and treatment plants.
The bar screen is constructed of bars installed
longitudinally in a channel, 1 to 1½ inches apart,
clear measurement, and should have a slope of
about 1:2. One inch screens collect 1 to 3 cubic
feet of screenings per million gallons of sewage.
The screens should also be constructed with an
overflow chamber to prevent stoppages (fig 2-6).
