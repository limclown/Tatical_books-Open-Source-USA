                                         FOREWORD

      This manual is published to provide interim guidance to commanders,
staff officers, and other personnel concerned with Judge Advocate Support under
the TASTA-70 concept of organization and operation. This information can be
utilized to facilitate reorganization under the TASTA Concept. Firm information
on the organizational structure and composition of unite will be as contained in
TOE's when published. Although the basic TASTA-70 Study has been approved
by Department of The Army, detailed doctrine contained in this Test Field
Manual is subject to further Department of The Army review and final approval.
