                       5. COMPUTER. The computer of the M9 director re-

                       ceives continuous present position data in the form
                       of rectangular coordinate (X,, Y,, and H,). (Note that
                       the director M9 is a linear speed director.) By utiliz-
                       ing these three values and their rates of change the
                       computer calculates the future position of the target.
                       It then calculates firing data for the future position
                       with respect to the guns. After incorporating ballistic
                       corrections and arbitrary corrections, it positions
                       selsyn transmitters to the corrected firing data (Af,
                        QE, and F) for transmission to the guns.
