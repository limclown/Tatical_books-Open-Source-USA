PREFACE

                                                                                            SCOPE

  This manual is designed to be used by all parachute riggers. It tells and shows how to rig the 120-millimeter
mortar in the trailer-mounted configuration with a 1 1/4-ton HMMWV-series truck for low-velocity airdrop from

a C-130, C-141, or C-5 aircraft. This manual also shows the 120-millimeter mortar rigged in a 1 1/4-ton

HMMWV<-series truck for low-velocity airdrop from a C-130, C-141, or C-5 aircraft.
