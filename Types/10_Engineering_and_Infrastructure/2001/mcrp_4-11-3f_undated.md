                                            FOREWORD

Marine Corps Reference Publication (MCRP) 4-11.3F, Convoy Operations Handbook,
addresses the fundamental principles required for the planning and execution of Marine
air-ground task force (MAGTF) convoy operations. This manual provides an overview
for convoy planning, organizations, and movement fundamentals, procedures, and
techniques.

MCRP 4-11.3F provides a source of reference for commanders and their staffs on
convoy operations and functions in support of the MAGTF. Specifically, it gives
general planning requirements, support requirements and considerations, and
procedures.

MCRP 4-11.3F consolidates and expands existing convoy operations doctrine and
supersedes portions of Fleet Marine Force Manual (FMFM) 4-9, Motor Transport,
(1992); FMFM 9-1, Marine Corps Tank Employment (1982); and FMFM 6-9, Marine
Artillery Support (1993).

Reviewed and approved this date.
