                            interconnecting cables.

Shelter facility— A shelter, modified to contain (but notinclude) components and interconnecting cables.

                                   The shelter facility contains a completely installed ac power distribution system,
                                    equipment racks secured to the floor and walls, and signal wiring, but does not
                                    include the communications equipment.
