The following information, published on Order No. 21506-Phila-55, supplements TM 11-297/TO
16-30VRC19-5, 25 August 1954. This supplement covers all serial numbers of the equipment.

Personnel using this equipment and having custody of the technical manual will enter suitable notations
beside each affected paragraph and figure in the technical manual to indicate the presence of this supple-
mentary information.

    References in the technical manual to specific serial numbers of equipments apply only to units made
on Order No. 11661-Phila-52, unless otherwise specified.

    Changes which were made only on Order No. 21506-Phila-55 in Radio Receiver R-894/U, Radio
Transmitter T-278/U, Power Supplies PP-867/U, PP-868/U, and PP-869/U, and Dynamotor-Power
Supplies DY-93/G, DY-98/G, and DY-100/U will be found in the manual, supplement, and addendas
applicable to the specific component.

    Changes which were made in radio set components not covered in separate technical manuals (such
as Radio Set Control C-847/U and Electrical Equipment Cabinet CY-938A/VRC) will be found in
Technical Manual TM 11-297 as amended by this supplement.
