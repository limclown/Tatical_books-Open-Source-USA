       DANGEROUS VOLTAGES EXIST IN THE EQUIPMENT

                                 DON’T TAKE CHANCES!

       Low-voltage, high-amperage current is present at output connections
and at connections inside the equipment. Do not touch connections or re-
move the cabinet cover until equipment is removed from power source.
Deenergize the power supply before connecting or disconnecting the equip-
ment to be powered and before performing any maintenance.

       Be careful when working on the 115- or 230-volt ac line connections.
Serious injury or DEATH may result from contact with these terminals.
