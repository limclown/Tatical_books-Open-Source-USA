SAFETY NOTICES

HYDROGEN GAS USED IN THE BALLOON IS HIGHLY INFAMMABLE. NO
SMOKING OR FIRE SHOULD BE PERMITTED IN THE VICINITY WHEN THE
BALLOON IS NEAR THE GROUND BEING INFLATED, DEFLATED, OR
MOORED.

PERSONNEL MUST NOT WEAR OUTER WOOLEN CLOTHING OF ANY SORT
WHEN INFLATING OR DEFLATING THE BALLOON. STATIC ELECTRICITY
PRODUCED BY THE RUBBING OF WOOL MAY IGNITE LEAKING HYDRO-
GEN GAS.

DURING THUNDERSTORMS PERSONNEL SHOULD STAND CLEAR OF AN-
TENNA WIRES. ANTENNAS A AND B WILL CARRY STATIC DISCHARGES
TO GROUND THROUGH THEIR TERMINATING RESISTANCE, BUT THE
ANTENNA WIRE IS NOT HEAVY ENOUGH TO CARRY A DIRECT LIGHTNING
STROKE SAFELY TO GROUND. THE ANTENNA WIRE MAY BE FUSED BY
THE STROKE WHICH MAY THEN JUMP TO NEARBY OBJECTS OR PER-
SONNEL. USE CAUTION.

DO NOT GRASP OR HOLD BALLOON OR KITE FLYING LINE DURING A
THUNDERSTORM IF WINCH IS MOUNTED IN A TRUCK. THE LINE IS. AN
INSULATOR WHEN DRY BUT MAY BECOME A CONDUCTOR WHEN WET.
IF WINCH IS STAKED TO GROUND NO DANGER EXISTS AND LINE MAY
BE GRASPED.

DO NOT TOUCH ANTENNA    WIRES WHEN THE   TRANSMITTER  IS IN OPER-
ATION. RADIO-FREQUENCY  VOLTAGE PRESENT  ON THE WIRE  MAY CAUSE
BURNS.

BE CAREFUL NOT TO INSTALL ANTENNAS A OR B NEAR OVERHEAD HIGH
TENSION LINES. WIND MAY CAUSE THE BALLOON OR KITE TO CARRY
ANTENNA WIRES ACROSS THESE HIGH TENSION LINES, CAUSING THE
ANTENNA AND ENTIRE RADIO STATION TO ASSUME POTENTIAL OF THE
LINES. INSTALL AT LEAST 2000 FEET FROM ALL HIGH TENSION LINES.

FOLLOW DIRECTIONS CAREFULLY WHEN INSTALLING OR USING THESE
ANTENNA ASSEMBLIES.
