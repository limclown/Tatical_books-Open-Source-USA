                     8. OPERATING PRECAUTIONS.

                        a. Never operate the inverter without a load. Be sure to disconnect the

                     inverter whenever the a-c load is not being used.

                        b. Never operate the inverter with a load of greater wattage input
                     rating than the maximum output of the inverter. This will cause the

                     fuse to blow, and will shorten the life of the inverter.

                         ce. Do not use the inverter longer than 3 or 4 hours at a time when
                     the operating load exceeds 100 watts.

                        d. To minimize interference in radio equipment using the inverter as
                     an a-c source, be sure to ground the radio and equip it with a good
                     aerial. Locate the radio and aerial lead-in as far from the inverter as
                     practicable. Excessive noise in the radio may further be minimized by
                     reversing the plug in the inverter output receptacle. -
