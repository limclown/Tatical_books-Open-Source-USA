4.1. TEST UNIT I-176 (Added).

   a. Test Unit I-176 is an instrument designed to measure resistance,
alternating current (a-c), direct current. (d-c), and voltage over a wide
range of values. The complete details of the operation and main-
tenance of this instrument are covered in TM 11-2626. Batteries
BA-2 and BA-30 are supplied with this instrument but must be
installed in the test set before use.

   b. There are five a-c voltage ranges, covering 0 to 5 volts, 0 to 25
volts, 0 to 100 volts, and 0 to 1,000 volts; each has a sensitivity of
1,000 ohms per volt.

   ce. The five d-c voltage ranges, providing a choice of either 1,000-
or 20,000-ohm-per-volt sensitivity, are 0 to 5 volts, 0 to 25 volts, 0
to 100 volts, 0 to 250 volts, and 0 to 1,000 volts, An additional
high-voltage range of 0 to 5,000 volis has a sensitivity of 20,000 ohms
per volt.

5.1 (Added.) Oscilloscope TS-239/UP

The oscilloscope, with its accessories and transit case, 1s shown in figure
5.1. This scope uses a 83-inch cathode-ray tube that is mounted at an
angle of 45° from the front panel. ‘The screen on the CRT (cathode-
ray tube) is viewed through a green plexiglas window in the front

       Figure 5.1. Oscilloscope TS-239/UP.

2                         ,                                      AGO 1856B

panel. The image of an illuminated orange-colored scale reflected from
the surface of the window appears to coincide with the CRT screen,
thus producing a reference scale free from parallax and differing in
color from the signal image. The scope is of the start-stop type in
which the horizontal sweep is started independently by each signal
pulse so that such pulses do not have to occur periodically to be viewed
satisfactorily. Sweep speeds are available from about 1 to 100,000
microseconds. Any portion of sweeps nominally over 10 microseconds
may be delayed and expanded for more detailed examination of part
of thesignal. Vertical amplification is uniform over a wide frequency

band so that fast-rising and flat-topped pulses from about 0.2 to 5,000
microseconds may be adequately displayed. Complete technical data
with operating and servicing instructions are contained in Handbook
of Operating and Maintenance Instructions AN 16-35TS239-3.,
