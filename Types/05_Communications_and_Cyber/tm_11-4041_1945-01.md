                            SAFETY NOTICE

   There is no chance of shock on the outside of ‘this equipment when it is in
operation. However, voltages dangerous to life aré present at various points
within the equipment. Always observe all safety regulations. Do not change
tubes or make adjustments inside the equipment with high voltage supply on.
Do not depend upon door switches or interlocks for protection. Always shut
cown motor generators or other power equipment. Under certain conditions
dangerous potentials may exist in circuits even with power controls off. This
is due to charges retained by capacitors. To avoid casualties, always remove
power, discharge, and ground circuits before touching them.

   If you install, operate, and maintain this or similar equipment you must be
prepared to give first aid. Your own life and the life of others may depend
upon this.
