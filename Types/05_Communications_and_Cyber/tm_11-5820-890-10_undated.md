                                            Section I. INTRODUCTION

1. SCOPE
This publication provides overprinted DA Forms 2062 (Hand Receipt) which list the contents of Components of
End Items (COEI) and Additional Authorization List (AAL) items for Radio Sets AN/PRC-119A/D/F, AN/VRC-
87A/C/D/F, AN/VRC-88A/D/F, AN/VRC-89A/D/F, AN/VRC-90A/D/F, AN/VRC-91A/D/F and AN/VRC-92A/D/F.

2. GENERAL
     a. Sections II through XXIV are the overprinted DA Forms 2062 listing contents of COEI’s and AAL’s,

extracted from TM 11-5820-890-10-8. Within each COEI and AAL the components are listed in alphabetical
order.

     b. The overprinted DA Form 2062 will eliminate manual preparation of the form and will assist organizations
in the inventorying and accounting for property as required by AR 710-2.

     c. Local reproduction of the overprinted DA Form 2062 is authorized. However, organizations will comply
with local policies in the reproduction of DA Form 2062 by office copying equipment, field printing plant or
duplicating facilities.

     d. Additional copies of this publication may be requisitioned from the US Army Publications Distribution
Center, 2800 Eastern Boulevard, Baltimore, MD 21220-2896 in accordance with the procedures in Chapter 12,
AR 25-30.

3. EXPLANATION OF BLOCKS AND COLUMNS (DA FORM 2062)
Refer to DA Pam 710-2-1, Chapter 6, for additional information required to complete DA Form 2062.

     a. FROM. Enter the organization which issues the property.
     b. TO. Enter the Unit Identifier Code (UIC) and the hand receipt file number of the unit/personnel receiving
the property.
     c. HAND RECEIPT NUMBER. Enter a locally designated number. Use it to post the location of property in
the property book.
     d. END ITEM STOCK NUMBER. Contains the National Stock Number (NSN) of the end item covered by the
hand receipt.
     e. END ITEM DESCRIPTION. Contains the end item short title.
     f. PUBLICATION NUMBER. Contains the TM number of the technical manual containing the operator/crew
instructions for the equipment.
     g. PUBLICATION DATE. Date of publication of the operator/crew technical manual.
     h. QUANTITY. Quantity of the end item covered by the hand receipt.
     i. STOCK NUMBER (Column a). National stock number of the item described. Items without stock numbers
should be requisitioned by Commercial and Government Entity Code (CAGEC) and part number with exception
data in accordance with AR 710-2.
     j. ITEM DESCRIPTION (Column b). Identifies the item contained in the COEI and AAL. It also contains
nomenclature and serial/USA number (if applicable) that are used in identifying and controlling the item. Serial
number is to be inserted and initialed on all copies by the hand receipt holder.

    k. * (Column c). The Accounting Requirements Code (ARC) is entered here. One of the following codes will

apply:

                                                                 NOTE

                           The accounting requirements code (ARC) refers to property
                           accountability, not to disposal authority. Disposition action and authority
                           for an unserviceable item is governed by the recoverabiltiy code (5th
                           position of the SMR code) as indicated in the applicable repair parts and
                           special tools list (RPSTL). The ARC’s and recoverability codes are listed
                           in the Army Master Data File (AMDF).
