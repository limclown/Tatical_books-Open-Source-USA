9. MEANING OF PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE.E.

    -_                                             -= ~             -
    Preventive maintenance may be defined as a systematic                                          series of opera

tions performed periodically on equipment in order to maintain top efficiency

in performance, to minimize unwanted interruptions in service, and to

eliminate major break-downs. To appreciate the meaning of the term pre

ventive maintenance, it is necessary to distinguish between preventive main

tenance, trouble shooting and repair. The primary function of preventive

maintenance is to prevent major break-downs and the consequent necessity
of repair. In sharp contrast, the primary function of trouble shooting and

repair is to locate and correct existing defects. The importance of preventive

maintenance cannot be overemphasized. The usefulness of an entire mete

orological system depends upon each piece of meteorological equipment

in the system being ready to operate at peak efficiency when needed. Con-

logical equipment maintain their equipment properly.
