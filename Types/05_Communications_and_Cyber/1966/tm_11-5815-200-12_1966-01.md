has the lowest numerical designation.

   ce. Line current for any particular signal line in
a teletypewriter system usually is supplied by the
station in the network that has the transmitting
device on that signal line. If two or more stations
have transmitting devices on the signal line, the
station of highest authority usually supplies the
line current. Two or more stations may supply
current when necessary because of excessive line
leakage or other line factors.
