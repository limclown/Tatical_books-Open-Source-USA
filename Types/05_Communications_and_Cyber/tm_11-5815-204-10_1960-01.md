                                   WARNING

            DANGEROUS VOLTAGES EXIST IN THIS EQUIPMENT
       The voltages used in this equipment are high enough to cause death.
When the transmitter is operating, high power is present at the antenna
and on the antenna lead-in wire. Be careful not to come in contact. with
the antenna or antenna circuit; severe burns and shock may result.
