                                                                detector-searchlight section requires the intelligent care and

                                                                tained in serviceable condition in accordance with the best
                                                                electrical and mechanical equipment practice. Such main-

                                                                elapse between successive oilings. The various parts of the

                                                                equipment should be oiled as necessary.

                                                                cleaning solutions supplied with the equipment should be
                                                                used, and only clean cotton pads passed in a radial movement
