Justification. This interim change provides procedures for the proper
application of tiedown straps used to secure nuclear weapons and components
on board US Army helicopters. This guidance is safety-related and is required
to prevent the inadvertent loosening of the tiedown straps during flight.

will be destroyed at that time unless \ooner rescinded or superseded by a
permanent change.
