This interim change is forwarded to the field to eliminate or modify a
policy, procedure, or other specification that could result in loss of
life, personal injury, or destruction of property; expires 1 year from
date of publication and will be destroyed at that time unless sooner
superseded by a formal printed change; is being distributed by 1st class
mail through the publications pinpoint distribution system to all holders
of FM 55-375; is, as an interim measure, issued in other than page-for-
page format; and will be included in FM 55-375 when revised.

Page 2-1. Paragraph 2-ld(3) is added:

2-ld(3). The warhead section faces forward.

Page 2-1. Paragraph 2-2b is amended to delete "Tiedowns and slings will
be inspected by a parachute rigger at least every 6 months. Under no
condition will a sling be used beyond the 6-month date without inspection
and recertification by a parachute rigger."

Page 3-1. Paragraph 3-If is superseded as follows:

       3-lf. The warhead section (front end of container) must be faced
forward for internal air transport. (Tiedown diagrams in this manual
show the container facing forward.) Container center of balance is
approximately 83 inches (2.11 m) from the aft end and is marked on the
container.
